# The fountain at the way in

Every floor lets the heroes in round a fountain that mends whoever stands near it — the heroes, and the dungeon's
own as well. Built from data and one module, with nothing added to the engine.

## What it is

- **A template** (`data/props/fountain.duke`): an `Object` with no body (nothing shoots it), a cylinder the width
  of its basin (it is walked round — the engine stamps it into the path grid as it does every immobile shape),
  and a `FountainUpdate` module.
- **The mending** (`level/FountainUpdate`): once a second, everything living within its reach is given back a share
  of its own maximum health and mana — 5% each, within 36 units. A share rather than points, so it is worth the same
  on every floor and to every hero; a pulse rather than a trickle, so the client's heal numbers say what it does.
  Deterministic: counted on the world's frame, each gift a whole number of points.
- **The look**: each theme's `ThemeMonster Fountain` names the model (Poly by Google, CC BY 3.0, lifted so its
  foot stands at y = 0) and the effect it carries.
- **The water** (`Effect FountainWater`): layers a thing carries for as long as it stands — TRAILs let out at its
  feet, a Height up — set at the model's own tiers: a jet out of the top, spilling from the little cup into the
  middle bowl and from there into the basin, mist off the basin, glints of the mending, and a cold light about as
  wide as its reach.

## Where it stands

`Run.WayIn` names the template; empty is none. `Spawner` puts it at the floor's way in, or the nearest cell to it
with open floor two cells all round and nobody standing in its three-by-three; a floor with none near goes
without. Props that fell inside its three-by-three are not laid. The heroes come in on the nearest open floor
beside it and the rest of a party beside the first, so they arrive together on one side of it.
