# Act 1 — start to the turning, as one playable chain

Phase 1, item 1 of `docs/plan/2026-10-15-big-plan.md`; the story is `docs/plan/2026-10-01-campaign.md` (the queen's
variant). Built on the story scripting (`2026-10-08-story-scripting.md`), people and trade, the medallion and the
turning (`2026-10-09-medallion.md`), and saving (`2026-10-08-saving.md`). All words are in the data
(`data/story/act1.duke`), in Uzbek. Every name and model the owner has not chosen is a **stand-in**, named so in the
data; they are listed at the end.

## The chain

| # | Joint | What it is | Ends by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `jang` | a new map: the field before the kingdom's wall | the enemy commander cut down → `MAP poytaxt` |
| 2 | `poytaxt` | a new map: the capital (gate yard, square, castle court, cellar) | the quest *Mashq* (reach the gate) → `MAP mashq` |
| 3 | `mashq` | the descent's generated floors, from depth 1 | `DEPTH 3` (two floors done) → `MAP poytaxt` |
| — | `poytaxt` again | the war council (flag `Trained`) | the quest *Urushga* (reach the gate) → `MAP urush` |
| 4 | `urush` | the descent's keep floors, from depth 3 (clear outside → key → gate → boss) | `DEPTH 5` (two keeps freed) → `MAP qora_qasr` |
| 5 | `qora_qasr` | a new map: the dark castle (yard, court, hall) | its lord's death → the turning → "1-qism tugadi" |

**The capital is its own small map, not the open world's Poytaxt.** The open world is Act 2's (the big plan's
Phase 2), and laying the whole 3.4 MB world for a few conversations and a gate would make Act 1's second joint its
heaviest. The world's Poytaxt stays as it is for Act 2.

### 1. The opening battle (`maps/jang`)

- `MAP_START`: the kingdom's soldiers stand in the line (`ALLY Soldier ×4`, `ALLY Militia ×3` — the KayKit knight
  and rogue looks, on the hero's side, fighting as monsters do); the army is called at the line (`CALL Skeleton`,
  `CALL Runner`); the cutscene `BattleOpening` (the line, the commander's and the enemy's lines, the army).
- Seen: a beam of light (`EFFECT SkyLight`) at `Landing`, the hero put in it (`PLACE`), the enemy commander thrown
  back to `Thrown` (`KNOCK`, dust), flag `HeroLanded`, the quest *Darvoza oldidagi jang*, the cutscene `Landing` (he
  is bewildered — he was training with Nilufar a moment ago; the commander: "you are a hero, take your sword").
- 90 frames on, the enemy commander rises and charges the landing (`CALL`). He cannot be hurt before the landing
  (his `Boss` phase 1, `DamageTaken = 0`) and cannot be killed by the hero after it (an `UNYIELDING` at 40 %).
- `BEATEN`: his line, the commander walks to him; 60 frames on she cuts him down (`KILL`); 30 more, the rest of the
  army breaks (`KILL` of each kind) and the cutscene `BattleOver` (the last hero summoned; the queen knows the way
  home). Seen → `MAP poytaxt`.

Played headless with no input at all, the soldiers and the hero's own brain win it (the hero ends a little hurt).

### 2. The capital (`maps/poytaxt`)

- First visit (`If FLAG_UNSET Trained`): the cutscene `CapitalShown` (the square, the commander: "follow me", the
  court) and the commander walks ahead to the court (`WALK`).
- The queen greets him as he comes near (her `Talk`, `QueenWelcome`): the kingdom's state, the dark knight, her gold
  and the medallion; kill the villain and you may go home; but train first. `HEARD` → `GIVE Medallion` (worn at once
  in the Tumor slot), `GOLD 100`, flag `Welcomed`; the conversation starts *Mashq*: reach the city gate → `MAP mashq`.
- The merchant (Savdogar Botir) stands in the square, as on the talk map.
- **Side quests:** the blacksmith's cellar (*Yerto'ladagi skeletlar*: three skeletons rise in the cellar when he asks;
  reward a Kuch qo'lqopi) and the old woman's ring (*Momoning uzugi*: the ring lies in the cellar; reward a Hayot
  tumori). The quest item `FamilyRing` is a QUEST thing in the act's own file, not the item tables.
- Back from the training (`If FLAG_SET Trained`): he is put in the court, the war council (`WarCouncilShown`: the
  queen feels the medallion filling; the commander lays out the war), the quest *Urushga* → the gate → `MAP urush`.

### 3. The training (`mashq`)

The descent's own floors under a name (the Campaign block's `Descents`): their bosses open the next floor as the
descent's do; nothing is won at the bottom. `MAP_START` starts *Mashq: eski qabrlar* (kill the second floor's boss).
`DEPTH 3` (the third floor laid): flag `Trained`, his line, `MAP poytaxt`.

### 4. The war (`urush`)

The descent's keep floors from depth 3: each is the keep mission (clear outside, take the key, give it to the gate,
the boss). `MAP_START`: *Bosib olingan yerlar* (the two keeps' bosses), the commander's line; `DEPTH 4`: her line on
the second keep; `DEPTH 5`: flag `LandsFreed`, her line, `MAP qora_qasr`.

### 5. The dark castle (`maps/qora_qasr`)

In by the yard, through the court its guards hold, up to the hall. `MAP_START`: the cutscene `CastleShown` (the lord
on his throne: "the queen sent you too?" — "I said that once"). The quest *Qora qasr*.
`DIED DarkLord`: the queen appears in a vision by the throne (`SPAWN Act1Queen`), the turning (`CURSE Act1Queen`:
the medallion leaves him for her hand, her glow, he darkens and goes over to the monsters' side, the run is won),
the run saved for Act 2 (`SAVE act1`), and the cutscene `TurningAct1` (him, her, her laugh and her lines, him again).
Seen → `WIN "1-qism tugadi"`: the banner, over the turning's own "Qahramon la'natlandi".

## What was added to the story

| | |
|---|---|
| `When = DEPTH`, Target a number | a floor laid at that depth (heard with `MAP_START`) — a campaign descent's measure |
| `Act = ALLY` (Count, Zone) | so many of a Monster at the zone **on the first hero's side**, grown to the floor's depth |
| `Act = PLACE` (Zone) | every hero at the zone's middle at once |
| `Act = KNOCK` (Target, Zone) | every one of a Monster standing thrown to the zone's middle at once |
| `Act = KILL` (Target) | every one of a Monster standing dies — a beaten `UNYIELDING` too |
| `Act = GOLD` (Count) | gold into the first hero's purse |
| `Act = SAVE` (Target, a slot) | the run written into that save slot now, won or not (`~/.duke-engine/saves/act1.duke`) |
| `WIN` after a win | on a story map already won (the turning), it says its words over the banner |
| `Campaign` block | the menu's row and blurb, the hero, `Descents` (name, FirstDepth) and `Joints` (map, and for a skip: Flags, Items, Experience, Gold) |

A MAP may name a campaign descent as if it were a map; a save on one loads back onto it (`Floors.named`).
**A death in the campaign** goes back to the last floor entered as he entered it (the checkpoint the autosave holds),
not to the opening battle with nothing — the roguelike's restart stays for the descent and the stages.

## Playing it

- **New game:** the front menu's *Yangi o'yin* → (alone) → the first row, *1-qism: Oxirgi qahramon*: the Knight,
  from the battle. The endless descent and the stages are the rows below it, as before; the act's maps also show in
  the stages list.
- **Skip to a joint (the owner's testing):** `./gradlew run --args="--act1=N"`, N from 1 to 5 — the game opens
  straight on joint N with the flags, the medallion, the experience and the gold the joints before it would have
  given (the Campaign block's `Joints`). 2 is the capital's first visit, 3 the training, 4 the war, 5 the castle.
- **Tests:** `Act1Test` plays the chain end to end headless, skips to every joint, loads a descent save, and dies
  back to the floor entered; `StageChoiceTest` takes the menu row.

## Deferred, and why

- The landing, the knock-back and the leap are jump cuts (`PLACE`, `KNOCK`): a move over frames is **E40**.
- Shots fly straight at an even speed: turning, pitch, zoom and easing are **E41**.
- "1-qism tugadi" is the banner: a real title card is **E42**.
- The commander "cuts him down" by walking to the landing and a `KILL` 60 frames later: where he was beaten is not
  known to a trigger, so she may strike from a few cells off.
- The level-20 gate before the war (the campaign's default) is not enforced: the hero cap is 15 today; the chain
  goes on when the story's steps are done.
- Act 2 reads `act1.duke`; nothing reads it yet.
