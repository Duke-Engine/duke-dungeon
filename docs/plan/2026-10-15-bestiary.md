# The bestiary — eight more families in the open world

The owner, 2026-10-15: "skeletons alone aren't enough, we need a lot of variety." 58 new monsters in
`data/units/bestiary_*.duke`, from two sources:

- **Quaternius** (poly.pizza): 41 models in `models/bestiary/`, converted by `art/monsters/export_bestiary.py`.
  CC0, except five under CC BY 3.0 (zombie ghoul, big-armed zombie, half zombie, witch, Animated Wizard). They
  are credited in CREDITS.md, `models/bestiary/License.txt` and the credits roll.
- **CGTrader Royalty Free**: 6 models the owner downloaded, converted by `art/monsters/export_licensed_monsters.py`
  into the git-ignored `licensed-assets/models/licensed/`. They are the golem (with earth, ice, shadow, fire and
  light skins), minotaur, troll, bandit, spider (three skins) and zombie.
  - Every block that wears one also names a stand-in from the free set (`Licensed = MonsterLook … End`), so a
    clone without the folder plays the same game.
  - The golems came with attacks only. Their idle, walk and death are retargeted from Quaternius' Universal
    Animation Library, and so is the zombie's death.

\* is a licensed look over a stand-in.

Golems turn blows aside (DRAGON_BLOOD, "Tosh teri"). Cave spiders throw a web that holds the hero (a stunning
SKILLSHOT). The game has no slow effect, so a zombie's bite drinks health instead.

Beside the camps, `WorldMapWriter.BEASTS` places 26 small groups after every camp, so nothing placed earlier
moves:

- grey wolves and wild bulls on the meadow
- foxes and stags in the forest
- the crypt's vermin round the crypt: crawling corpses, skull crawlers, bats, rats, the witch, cave spiders and
  the brood mother
- pirates and drowned sailors along the lake
- orcs in the highlands, yeti cubs in the snow, ruin rats and crowned shades at the ruins, imps by the castle

None of them stands within 70 cells of the city. None stands near the opening field, the corridor or the old
graves either: those are the places the Act 1 gates keep the hero in (`BestiaryTest`).

The crypt's vermin also come up in the descent. Rat and bat start at depth 2, the skull crawler and crawling
corpse at depth 3, and the cave spider at depth 4, each at a light Weight.

## Feel and sound

- Bosses get a HeavyBlow at about a fifth of their health and a Slam when they die. Elites and the big bodies
  get a Thud (`feel.duke`).
- Sounds are in `data/sounds/bestiary.duke`. They use clips the game already ships, picked by kind of creature
  (man, beast, orc, undead, bones, shade, bug, demon, golem, dragon). Every walker has footsteps on every ground.

## Clips

- Clips are Idle, Walk, Attack and Death, plus Run and Hit where the model has them.
- WalkStride and PreAttackFrames are measured by MonsterClipFitTest.
- A licensed attack is retimed so its blow lands when its stand-in's does, since one block's PreAttackFrames
  serves both looks.
- The kraken's tentacle had no walk and no death. It walks by its idle and shrinks into the lake as it dies.
