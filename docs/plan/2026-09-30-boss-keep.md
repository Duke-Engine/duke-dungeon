# The boss's keep

The owner's ask: every boss stands on a storey above the rest of the floor, walled in, with a gate in the middle of
the wall — and missions to lead the party there: kill everything outside, take the key the last of them leaves,
use it on the gate, kill the boss. It is three pieces of work, each with its own spec, plan and code, in this order
so the game is playable after every one:

1. **The keep** (this spec): the boss's walled court one storey up, its gate, its stair, and the road to it. Until
   there is a key, the gate opens when a hero walks up to it.
2. **Usable items**: a thing in the bag that does something when clicked — the key on a gate first, and after it
   items like a blink dagger (the `BLINK` skill effect) or illusions (`SUMMON`).
3. **Missions**: each floor's chain of goals from a data file, shown at the top of the screen with hints; the first
   chain is kill-all → key → gate → boss.

Nothing here needs the engine: storeys, stairs, masonry walls and a look per cell are all there already.

## What the player sees

At the far end of the floor a square keep stands a storey above the ground, walled all round in worked stone
whatever the biome. A straight road leads from one of the chambers to a flight of steps; at the top of the steps,
in the middle of the wall, a gate three cells wide. Behind it is the court: open floor, the boss in the middle and
its guard round it. The rest of the keep sits in the rock or the wood, so its walls are seen from inside the court
and from the road, not from the other side.

## Where it goes

The keep is placed once the cave is carved, in **solid rock**: its square and one cell round it must all be rock,
inside the map's border. So it takes no floor from anything and cuts no tunnel, and whatever was connected stays
connected.

- **Sizes** come from the file, largest first: `KeepSizes = [15, 13, 11, 9]`. Odd, so the court has a middle cell
  for the boss and the three-cell doorway has a middle cell for the gate. The court is the size less its wall: 13,
  11, 9 or 7 across.
- **Beside which chamber:** every size but the last is tried only beside the deepest chambers (at most one tunnel
  short of the deepest), largest first; failing that, the deepest chamber any size fits beside. Its road — the
  steps along the grid from the chamber's middle to the keep's, less half the keep — is no longer than
  `MaxRoomSpacing`, and beside a chamber the square with the shortest road wins. Chambers as deep go in room order,
  and squares with roads as short in reading order, so a seed names one keep.
- Measured over 800 floors of all six biomes: a keep fits on every one; 61% are 15 across, 23% 13, 14% 11 and 2.5%
  9; 83% stand beside the deepest chamber and all but two within one tunnel of it. Without the 9, one floor in the
  800 had no keep. (Measured with sizes 14, 12 and 10, trying the largest size first anywhere put about one keep in
  eight two or more tunnels short of the end, and trying the deepest chamber first made about one in seven the
  smallest size.)
- A floor where none fits keeps today's boss chamber — flat and open, with no gate. It did not happen once in the
  800; it is there so the generator can never fail. The cave is carved exactly as now — the chamber that would have
  been the boss's still gets no loops and no islands — so that floor is today's floor.

## How it is built

- **Walls:** the square's outer ring is rock. **Court:** the rest is floor on storey `1`.
- **Gate side:** the side whose outward direction points most nearly at the chamber the keep hangs off (ties in the
  order north, east, south, west). The three middle cells of that side's ring are floor on storey `1`: the doorway.
- **Stair:** the three cells just outside the doorway are `/` — a ramp, whose foot the engine puts on storey 0.
- **Road:** from the cell beyond the stair's middle, a straight tunnel of `CorridorWidth` to the chamber's middle,
  stamped after the cave is worn, so it is a built road rather than a worn one. It cannot cross the keep: it
  leaves the gate side heading outward — the side was chosen for it — and a straight line leaving a square
  outward never meets it again.
- **Ground:** the relief is drawn and held to its slope as now; then the keep, its stair and its doorway are set to
  the lowest height within two cells of them, and the slope rule is run once more. Nothing near the keep is lower
  than the keep, so it is never lowered and stays level; the ground round it is eased down to meet it, so the road
  to the stair is never a cliff.
- **The keep is a chamber** wherever chambers are counted: appended to `rooms` last, as the boss room, joined by
  one link to its chamber (a leaf, as the boss room has always been), on storey 1 in `roomStoreys`. Its biome is the
  one under its middle. Monsters are not scattered in it (the boss room never had any); the boss stands on its
  middle cell and its guard on the ring round it, as now; props may stand in the court as they did in the boss's
  chamber, which keeps them two cells off the walls and the doorway. No scenery is strewn on its cells.
- `GeneratedDungeon` carries the keep's walled square and its stair cells for the look; null where there is none.

## Its look

A new theme, `data/world/themes/keep.duke`: a masonry kit (the dungeon's own floor, wall slab and stairs, walls a
storey high), listed with the others but in neither `Biomes` nor `Themes`, so no floor or region is ever drawn in
it. The floor's map record answers the Keep look for every cell of the keep and its stair, and the biome's look
elsewhere. A wall is drawn by the look of the rock it stands against, so the ring is walled in stone on both of its
faces and the rock round it stays cliff or wood. A descent whose file lists no `Biomes` has no map of looks, and its
keep wears the floor's one theme.

## The gate

- `data/props/gate.duke`: a still thing, `STRUCTURE` and `SELECTABLE` (the pointer finds it, and later the key is
  aimed at it), no body, a `Box` geometry across the whole doorway.
- The generator lists it among the floor's props, on the doorway's middle cell, so it is spawned as props are. The
  spawner turns it across the doorway: along the axis whose cells two away are rock.
- **Until there is a key**, a module on it opens it when a living hero comes within its reach (`Reach` in its
  block). Opening swaps it for the template its block names (`Opens = OpenGate`): the gate with a shape goes, so the
  doorway can be walked at once, and in its place, turned as it was, stands the same model with no shape, swinging
  its two leaves open as it appears and staying open — its `open` clip played once and held on its last frame, the
  engine's `ClipMode.ONCE`, named in the Keep theme by a new `PlayOnce` line.
- **Its model** is the owner's: "Old Driveway Gate" by animatedheaven (CGTrader; free for commercial use with
  credit) — two stone pillars with a lamp on each and an iron gate of two leaves between them, 106 across and 70
  tall as modelled, with its colours in the file. The owner animated the leaves in Blender, one clip each
  (`left_open`, `right_open`, 2.5 seconds), resting closed; the game's copy has the two joined into one clip,
  `open`, by `art/models/join_clips.pl` — the same keys, so one clip opens both leaves. At a scale of 0.37 the iron
  gate fills the 30-unit doorway and the pillars stand in the wall either side of it, 26 units tall over a wall of
  10: a gate that stands above the keep. The Keep theme names it, so a different model is a line of data.

## Tests

- **Every floor has a keep** (seeds 0–199, all six biomes): 15, 13, 11 or 9 across; the last room and the boss room,
  a leaf; the boss on its middle cell; the guard inside the court; the gate on the doorway's middle cell, in the
  side facing its chamber.
- **Walkable only through the gate,** with the engine's own step rule (`PathGrid.canStep` over the map and its
  storeys): with the doorway closed no court cell is reached from the way in; with it open every one is. Every
  other chamber is reached either way (`StageCheck` finds no problem).
- **Storeys and ground:** court and doorway on 1, the stair `/`, all else 0; the keep's corners all one height; no
  cliff anywhere.
- **Look:** the floor's map record answers the Keep look on the keep's cells and the biome's off them;
  `DungeonTilesTest` loads and measures the Keep kit as it does every kit.
- **The gate in a world:** spawned across its doorway, nothing walks through it; a hero walking up opens it — the
  open gate standing where it stood, turned as it was — and then the doorway is walked. The Keep theme's `open`
  clip is on the model (`DungeonTilesTest`).
- **The same seed, the same keep;** the old generator tests hold where they did not assume the boss room was a
  chamber.

## Not in this part

The key, usable items and missions (parts 2 and 3). Stages are cut without a keep, as they are cut without biomes:
a stage's file has nowhere to keep its look (`Layout.mixesBiomes`), and a stage read back must be the floor it was
cut from. The two shipped stages are not redrawn, and the mission chain will skip a gate that is not there.
