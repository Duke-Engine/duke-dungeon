# Act 2 — the heroine, as quests in the open world

Phase 2, item 4 of `docs/plan/2026-10-15-big-plan.md`; the story is `docs/plan/2026-10-01-campaign.md` ("Act 2" and
"The end"), carried on from Act 1 (`docs/plan/2026-10-15-act1.md`). Every word is in the data
(`data/story/act2.duke`, `data/story/kampir.duke`, `data/units/act2.duke`), in Uzbek. Names and models the owner has
not chosen are **stand-ins**, listed at the end.

## The chain

### Act 1 carries over

Act 1 ends by writing its run to `~/.duke-engine/saves/act1.duke` (its `SAVE act1`). The Act 2 campaign block says
`Follows = act1`: a new Act 2 reads that save and carries its **flags** (what happened) and its **hero's level** —
kept as the flag `act1Level=N` — into the new run. In the hall the cursed hero is grown to that level (`GROW`): the boss
she must not kill is the player's own Act 1 hero. With no Act 1 save (Act 2 started straight from the menu, or
`--act2=N`), the act's `DefaultLevel` (10) stands in. Her own hero is new: the Rogue at level 1, not cursed.

### 1. The arrival

`MAP_START` (`If FLAG_SET Act2`, and only before she has arrived): the card *2-qism: Kamonchi*, the beam
(`SkyLight`) on the square, she falls from 140 up into it (`FALL`, dust and a knock of the camera), and the cutscene
`HeroineLands` (low and close). The coming is by the kingdom's summoning (the coordinator's, 2026-10-15), and she knows
the light: it is how he vanished. The commander walks up and speaks (`CommanderMeetsHer`): the last hero went to the
dark castle and never came back; ask the city. *Uni izlab*: talk to the elder.

The capital's people (`CapitalPeople`, a `Repeat` `MAP_START`) are put on the square every time the world is laid in
the act: the commander, the baker (*Novvoy Karim*), the guard (*Qorovul Jasur*), the elder (*Oqsoqol Hamid*).

### 2. The witch and the caged girl

The elder's word starts *Qorong'i o'rmondagi kampir*: talk to Yalmog'iz kampir at her hut. **Her price** (decided):
the necromancer who robs her wood's graves — kill him (a necromancer is put at her wood as the quest starts, whatever
became of the map's own), then come back. Paid, she tells the secret (`KampirsSecret`): she made the medallion for the
queen; the souls it drinks keep the queen young; the hero fills it and it drinks him; the cursed hero keeps the war
going so the kingdom keeps summoning heroes; kill the queen — but her castle is sealed until the conquered lands are
freed.

**The caged girl** (side, *Qafasdagi qiz*, decided): the necromancer sold her to the witch and carries the cage's key
— it is hers when he dies (`GIVE CageKey` on his `DIED`); given to the girl, she is free (her thanks, experience and a
ring). The cage opens (`REMOVE Damsel`): the empty cage stays swinging on its gibbet (`EmptyCage`, Inuciian's cage
with nobody in it, cut by `art/inuciian/export_models.py`), and she steps out (`FreedDamsel`, the Damsel's own uncaged
model) and walks to the capital, by the commander (`GirlHome`), where she thanks the heroine again when talked to. A
load lays her there, and the cage empty (`GirlStaysFree`).

### 3. Freeing the lands

The secret starts three MAIN quests, one a land. A land is freed when its fort's camp is **cleared**: the new
`When = CLEARED` (a zone that holds no creature of the dungeon's side any more, heard as one of them dies). Each:
the quest finished, its flag, her line, a **checkpoint**, and something changed —

- **Yashil dala:** a merchant comes back to the meadow (`SPAWN Merchant`, and again whenever the world is laid);
- **Toshli adir**, **Qumli dasht:** the freed folk's thanks (150 gold each).

The three flags together set `LandsFreed` (three `FLAG` triggers, each asking for the other two): her line, and
*Malika qasri*. A freed camp stays freed when the world is laid again (a load, a death): `CLEAR` takes its creatures
away unheard. Fast-travel towers are the world dressing's (branch world-dressing); when they land, a freed land can
open its tower here with one action.

### 4. The queen's castle

Coming into `Malika_qasri` before the lands are freed: her line, the gate sealed (`CastleShut`, `Repeat`). After: the
beam on the castle, the cutscene `GateOpens` (the camera drawn back and up), and `MAP hall`.

### 5. The hall, the end

The hall is the final fight as `docs/plan/2026-10-09-final-fight.md` built it. Added: its card (*Malika zali*), the
cursed hero grown to Act 1's level, and after its `TheEnd` (`WIN` *La'nat ko'tarildi*) — 3 s on, the card **Oxiri**;
then, 7 s on, the credits **roll** (`CREDITS`, E49): `data/story/credits.duke`, the makers from `CREDITS.md` grouped by
kind — code and design, the engine, heroes, monsters and movement, the world, castles, weapons, icons, sounds, music,
lettering — with its music cue (`music.victory`); rolled out or skipped (Esc, a
click), the window goes back to the front menu.

## What was added

| | |
|---|---|
| `Campaign` blocks, more than one | each act: `Name` (`act1`, `act2` — the debug arg and the end save's slot), `Follows`, `DefaultLevel`; a joint's `At` (the zone a skip puts her in) and `Quests` (started on a skip) |
| `Place` blocks | zones a story adds to a map from its own data (`Map`, `Zone` as a map's Zones line): the open world's file is WorldMapWriter's, so its act-2 places stay out of it |
| `When = CLEARED` (Target a zone) | the zone holds none of the dungeon's creatures any more |
| `Act = CLEAR` (Zone) | its dungeon creatures taken away, unheard |
| `Act = CHECKPOINT` | the run written now as the autosave and the checkpoint a death goes back to — progress on one great map |
| `Act = GROW` (Target, Count) | every one of a Monster grown to the carried level (`<Follows>Level=N`), or to Count |
| `Act = MENU` | the game ends: back to the front menu (`DungeonRun.onTheEnd`) |
| `Act = CREDITS` | the Credits block rolled with its music; the front menu once it has rolled (E49) |
| `Act = REMOVE` (Target) | every one of a Monster or a person taken away, unheard |

**No WorldMapWriter change**, and no change to `maps/world/world.map`: the act's people, places and story are data.

## Playing it

- **New game:** *Yangi o'yin* → alone → the second row, *2-qism: Kamonchi* (the first is Act 1). It reads
  `act1.duke` when there is one.
- **Skip:** `./gradlew run --args="--act2=N"`, N 1 to 5 — from the act's defaults (it does not read `act1.duke`).
- **Tests:** `Act2Test` plays the whole act headless from the landing to the front menu, carries an Act 1 save over,
  skips to every joint, keeps the castle shut until the lands are freed, and keeps a freed camp freed across a death.
