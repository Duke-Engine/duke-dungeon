# Mounts: a horse on the open world (2026-10-15)

The owner, 2026-10-15: horse riding on the big open world. Data: `data/world/mounts.duke` (the `Mount` block, the
horse, the three riding looks, its sounds, the whistle); Java: `mount/Mount` (the block), `run/Stable` (riding as
it is played). Frames are the simulation's, 30 a second.

## The owner's rules, and what the game does

| Rule | Done as |
|---|---|
| A horse is bought at a stable in the capital, or given by the story | owning a horse is carrying the **whistle** (`ot`, *Ot hushtagi*, a Potion that is never eaten, stack 1): the capital's stable keeper **Otboqar Rustam** sells it for 300 gold; the story gives it as any thing is given. Kept in the bag, so it is saved with the bag |
| Summoned with a key near the hero after a short call | **T** (free in both schemes: classic Q W E R, F A S D; modern Q E R F, W A S D, Space; the screens' C L M, the tracker's J, the fire's G, H P, 1-4 are all elsewhere; `validateMount` refuses a clash). The horse neighs and runs in from 70 units away, from the first of eight ways round him (in a fixed order, so every machine picks the same) that is open ground; with none open it appears beside him in a puff of dust |
| Only outdoors on the open world | the whistle is refused (said, nothing spent) on a map not in `Maps` (`[world]`: the descent, the keeps, the capital's own map, the throne hall are all refused) and inside a `NoRideZones` zone (the city and the five villages) |
| Mounting: a key or RMB on the horse, a 0.5-0.8 s clip | **T** with the horse within `Reach` (14) casts his `MOUNT` skill (each hero's, `Key = T`, data/units); so does a right-click on the horse (the `Ride` cursor, action's E). He walks up to it first; the skill's `WindUpFrames` (18, 0.6 s) play his `Gather`; a step or a blow calls it off (the engine's, E89). Then he rides the horse on its `Saddle` bone (E88) |
| While mounted: ≈2× speed, WASD and clicks | the skill's `BoostPercent` (100) through the engine's `SpeedModifier`; shown on the buff row as `MOVE_SPEED` (the Mount block's skill, `Yurish tezligi`). A click-to-move is held to his walk by the engine for now (E89's note) |
| No attacks or skills while mounted; pressing them dismounts | a skill cast while he rides gets him off and is not cast (`Dungeon`, the modern Space roll too); an attack ordered gets him off (`Stable`: `SkillBook.dismount`); the book holds his weapon's fire while he rides |
| Taking a hit dismounts | the skill's `EndsOnHurt` |
| Towns, interiors, dungeons auto-dismount | the skill's `NotIn` (refused there, nothing spent) against the zones `Stable.zonesAt` names (`GameLogic.setZones`): the city, the five villages, and `Ichkari` on every map not in `Maps`; riding into one gets him off; the horse stands where he got off |
| The horse stays where left | dismounting lays the horse beside him (a neutral creature with no body: nothing fights it); T calls it back to him later, from wherever it stands, and it stops as it comes within Reach of him |
| Visuals | the real horse carries him on its `Saddle` bone (`GameObject.rideOn`, E88), lifted off the ground (AIRBORNE) and kept under him while he rides; he is drawn as `<Hero>Riding` (the skill's `Look`): his own model, ModelScale his over the horse's 4, Facing 0, playing `Ride_Idle`/`Ride_Gallop` (`animations/characters/riding_<hero>.glb`, art/mounts/export_riders.py) and holding his bow or staff (a look holds one thing). The horse is Quaternius' "Horse" (CC0, poly.pizza qvTrSG9pZF) |
| Sounds | `mount.call` (a whinny, on the whistle); his footsteps while he rides are `walking.<Hero>Riding.<surface>`, the hooves (E88); the horse's own `walking.Horse.<surface>` as it runs in |
| Save / load | the whistle in the bag is owning one; `Mounted = Yes` in the save puts him back on his horse when the world is laid again -- where a load puts him: the last tower he found rides on, the city (no tower found yet) is on foot |
| Fast travel | the towers work as before, mounted or not; mounted travellers on the roads (E52) later |

## The numbers (data/world/mounts.duke)

| What | Value |
|---|---|
| Key | T, `Ot` on the Settings keys page |
| Riding speed | +100% of his walking speed (knight 21 -> 42, rogue 27 -> 54, mage 23.7 -> 47) |
| Mount clip | 18 frames (0.6 s), his `Gather` |
| Call distance | 70 units: past his sight, so the horse runs in from off the screen |
| Horse's run | 48 a second (MoveUpdate of the `Horse` template) |
| Gallop's stride | 22 units a play (`WalkStride` of the horse and each riding look, as `MonsterClipFitTest` measures the gallop) |
| Reach to mount | 14 from the horse's middle |
| Whistle's price | 300 gold, one in the stock of the stable keeper **Otboqar Rustam**, inside the city's east wall (`WorldMapWriter`, one line of `maps/world`) |

## Engine requests (docs/plan/2026-09-29-engine-requests.md)

E88 and E89 landed (engine 0.8.0, `e86-89-hour-mounts`). Left: a click-to-move is held to his walk (`MoveUpdate.giveWay`).

## Not done

- No mounted charge (owner: "only if cheap"): a skill of its own, later.
- No dismount clip: getting off is at once, with a puff of dust; the mount clip is his reach (`Gather`).
- The horse is not saved where it stood: a loaded world has no horse until he whistles.
- The rogue rides with her bow and no dagger, the mage with his staff and no book: a look holds one thing.
- No blow is struck from the saddle: an attack gets him off first.
- The horse's footprint is his own (radius 5): a long horse may clip a wall corner while he rides.

## Tests

`run/MountTest`: the whistle calls a horse from 70 away, T gets him up onto it, he is drawn as his riding look at twice
his walk and walks again once off; a blow, a skill and the city each get him off (the skill is not cast); no whistle,
the city or the descent: no horse; the save keeps him riding; the stable keeper sells the whistle in the capital.
