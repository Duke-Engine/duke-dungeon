# Mounts: a horse on the open world (2026-10-15)

The owner, 2026-10-15: horse riding on the big open world. Data: `data/world/mounts.duke` (the `Mount` block, the
horse, the three riding looks, its sounds, the whistle); Java: `mount/Mount` (the block), `run/Stable` (riding as
it is played). Frames are the simulation's, 30 a second.

## The owner's rules, and what the game does

| Rule | Done as |
|---|---|
| A horse is bought at a stable in the capital, or given by the story | owning a horse is carrying the **whistle** (`ot`, *Ot hushtagi*, a Potion that is never eaten, stack 1): the capital's stable keeper **Otboqar Rustam** sells it for 300 gold; the story gives it as any thing is given. Kept in the bag, so it is saved with the bag |
| Summoned with a key near the hero after a short call | **T** (free in both schemes: classic Q W E R, F A S D; modern Q E R F, W A S D; the screens' C L M, the tracker's J, the fire's G, H P, 1-4 are all elsewhere). The horse neighs and runs in from 70 units away, from the first of eight ways round him (in a fixed order, so every machine picks the same) that is open ground; with none open it appears beside him in a puff of dust |
| Only outdoors on the open world | the whistle is refused (said, nothing spent) on a map not in `Maps` (`[world]`: the descent, the keeps, the capital's own map, the throne hall are all refused) and inside a `NoRideZones` zone (the city and the five villages) |
| Mounting: a key or RMB on the horse, a 0.5-0.8 s clip | **T** with the horse within `Reach` (14) mounts; so does a right-click on the horse (the `Ride` cursor). He walks up to it, then stands 18 frames (0.6 s) at it playing his `Gather` reach; a step, a blow or another order calls it off. Then the horse is gone into him: he is drawn as his riding look |
| While mounted: ≈2× speed, WASD and clicks | his legs are set to his walking speed × (100 + `SpeedPercent`)/100 every frame he rides (100: twice). The MoveTo of a click and E74's `MoveAlong` both walk at that speed. The riding look's walk is the horse's gallop, paced by `WalkStride` |
| No attacks or skills while mounted; pressing them dismounts | a skill cast while he rides dismounts him and is not cast; an attack ordered (`isTargetOrdered`) dismounts him; a target his weapon picks for itself is dropped every frame he rides (`holdFire`), so riding past a camp does not unseat him |
| Taking a hit dismounts | any fall in his health from one frame to the next unseats him (a frame's heal from a meal is far smaller than a blow) |
| Towns, interiors, dungeons auto-dismount | entering a `NoRideZones` zone, or a map not in `Maps`, dismounts him; the horse stands where he got off |
| The horse stays where left | dismounting lays the horse beside him (a neutral creature with no body: nothing fights it); T calls it back to him later, from wherever it stands |
| Visuals | `GameObject.drawAs` (the engine's disguise, out of the checksum and not saved): his template is drawn as `<Hero>Riding`, ONE combined model per hero -- the horse's rig with the hero's mesh in a riding pose skinned to the horse's back, so the gallop drives both (art/mounts/export_riders.py). The horse is Quaternius' "Horse" from his Ultimate Animated Animals (CC0, poly.pizza qvTrSG9pZF): Idle, Walk, Gallop, Eating, Death, Idle_HitReact_Left |
| Sounds | `mount.call` (a whinny, on the whistle), `mount.gallop` every stride while he rides and moves, `walking.Horse.<surface>` for the horse running in (grass, forest, mud, sand; ground elsewhere). The mounted hero's footsteps stay his own until the engine sounds by the look drawn (E82) |
| Save / load | the whistle in the bag is owning one; `Mounted = Yes` in the save puts him back on his horse when the world is laid again |
| Fast travel | the towers work as before, mounted or not; mounted travellers on the roads (E52) later |

## The numbers (data/world/mounts.duke)

| What | Value |
|---|---|
| Key | T, `Ot` on the Settings keys page |
| Riding speed | +100% of his walking speed (knight 21 -> 42, rogue 27 -> 54, mage 23.7 -> 47) |
| Mount clip | 18 frames (0.6 s), his `Gather` |
| Call distance | 70 units: past his sight, so the horse runs in from off the screen |
| Horse's run | 48 a second (MoveUpdate of the `Horse` template) |
| Reach to mount | 14 from the horse's middle |
| Whistle's price | 300 gold, one in the stable keeper's stock |

## Engine requests (docs/plan/2026-09-29-engine-requests.md)

- **E82** -- a rider on a mount: one unit drawn riding another's bone, both animating (the mount's gallop, the rider's
  own riding clips and his upper-body blows), his bars and pick on him; and the client's unit sounds (walking, hurt,
  died) keyed by the template a unit is drawn as (`looksAs`), not its own. Today: one combined model per hero through
  `drawAs`, and a gallop the game plays as an event.
- **E83** -- mounting in the skill system: a `MOUNT` skill shape (or the least of it, a `MOVE_SPEED` buff the book holds
  and `HeroProgress` multiplies into his legs), with a cast time (the mount clip), ended by a blow, a cast or an
  attack, and its look lent through `drawAs`; and zones of a map a skill is refused and ended in. Today: `run/Stable`
  sets his legs' speed every frame he rides and watches his health, his weapon and the zones itself.

## Not done

- No mounted charge (owner: "only if cheap"): it is a skill once E83 is.
- No dismount clip: getting off is at once, with a puff of dust; the mount clip is his reach (`Gather`).
- The horse is not saved where it stood: a loaded world has no horse until he whistles.
- The rider in the combined model holds still on the horse's back (the horse's bones move him); his own riding clips
  wait for E82. A blow on him unseats him first, so he never dies on horseback.
- The horse's footprint is his own (radius 5): a long horse may clip a wall corner while he rides.
