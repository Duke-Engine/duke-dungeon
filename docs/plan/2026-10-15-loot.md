# Items worth finding (big plan, phase 1, item 2)

Diablo-like loot for Last Hero: bases for every worn slot, four rarities with random affixes, drops by level and
kind, potions on a belt bound to 1–4, coloured item cards that compare with what is worn, the merchant's stock drawn
from the same tables, and saving that keeps every roll. The owner had no time; every decision below is a stand-in he
may change in data (`data/world/loot.duke`), none needs Java.

## What an item is

A generated item is one engine `Loot`, as every item is. Its **id** says everything needed to make it again:

    keng_qilich-R7-1k3x9a      base, rarity letter + item level, seed (base 36)
    ajdar_tishi-E6-0           an epic: the unique's name instead of a base
    hayot_shishasi-P1-2b7      a potion stack

The rarity letters are C, M, R, E (common, magic, rare, epic) and P (potion). Rolling an item is a pure function of
its base, rarity, level and seed (`loot/Items.gear`), so a save keeps only the id and the item comes back with every
affix exactly as it was rolled. Ids are unique per item, so two alike never join (the engine's three-alike join is for
the old attribute items only). Ids that are not of this form are the old `LootItem`s and load as before.

## Rarity

| | word | colour | affixes | name |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| common | Oddiy | grey | none | the base's |
| magic | Sehrli | blue | 1–2 | the affixes' prefixes before the base: *O'tkir qonxo'r qisqa qilich* |
| rare | Noyob | gold | 3 (4 with E43) | two words from two lists: *Qora bo'ri* |
| epic | Afsonaviy | purple | fixed | a hand-written unique with a lore line |

Rarity is drawn per source, out of 1000 (`ItemRules`): an ordinary monster 700/250/45/5, an elite (Brute, Champion,
Warden, Reaper) 380/430/160/30, a boss 0/450/430/120, a chest placed on a map 250/450/260/40, the merchant
550/380/70/0. An epic whose unique is above the item level falls back to rare.

## Bases (35)

Each base has a slot, a figure (the slot's kind), a level it is found from, a value range and a price. The figure by
slot: Qurol — damage %, Zirh — armour %, Dubulg'a — health, Etik — agility, Tumor — mana, Uzuk — one attribute rolled
among strength, agility and intelligence.

- **Qurol (12):** xanjar, qisqa qilich, bolta, kamon, sehrgar hassasi, keng qilich, gurzi, arbalet, uzun qilich, uzun
  kamon, jang boltasi, egri qilich (4–6 % at level 1 up to 16–20 % at 12).
- **Zirh (6):** po'stin, charm kamzul, sehrgar libosi, zanjirli sovut, temir sovut, plastinka sovut (2–4 % to 9–12 %).
- **Dubulg'a (6):** teri qalpoq, charm qalpoq, zanjirli dubulg'a, temir dubulg'a, shoxli dubulg'a, plastinka dubulg'a
  (15–25 to 80–100 health).
- **Etik (4):** choriq, charm etik, zanjirli etik, temir etik (1–2 to 4–6 agility).
- **Tumor (4):** marjon, oltin nishon, zumrad tumor, qadimiy tumor (10–15 to 38–50 mana).
- **Uzuk (3):** oltin uzuk, muz uzuk, olmos uzuk (1–2 to 4–6 of an attribute).

Each draws one of the icon library's pictures, framed in its rarity's colour: `art/icons/ItemIcons.java` paints a
`loot` group, every row in all four frames, into `icons/items/loot/<icon>_<rarity>.png`.

## Affixes (9)

From data (`Affix` blocks): a figure, the slots it may roll on, the adjective a magic item is named with, and its
range at item level 1 and at 15 (`Low = [2, 12]`, `High = [5, 24]`: the roll is between the two, both drawn
straight between their ends by level).

| affix | figure | slots | level 1 | level 15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| O'tkir | damage % | Qurol | 2–5 | 12–24 |
| Mustahkam | armour % | Zirh | 1–2 | 4–8 |
| Baquvvat | health | Dubulg'a | 10–20 | 60–110 |
| Donishmand | mana | Tumor | 6–12 | 30–55 |
| A'lo | the base's attribute | Etik, Uzuk | 1–2 | 4–7 |
| Tez | attack speed % | Qurol, Etik, Uzuk, Tumor | 3–6 | 8–16 |
| Shifobaxsh | health a second | Zirh, Dubulg'a, Etik, Tumor, Uzuk | 1–2 | 3–6 |
| Ilhomli | mana a second | Qurol, Dubulg'a, Tumor, Uzuk | 1–2 | 2–5 |
| Qonxo'r | lifesteal % | every slot | 2–3 | 4–8 |

**How they reach the hero — what the engine's `Loot` can carry.** A `Loot` carries one figure (`Kind`/`Value`), one
extra (`Extra`/`ExtraValue`) and the passives it lends (`Skills`). So an item carries:

- its base figure, with an affix of the same figure folded into it (O'tkir on a sword adds to its damage %);
- one extra-figure affix (Tez, Shifobaxsh or Ilhomli);
- one lifesteal affix, as a lent passive `LIFESTEAL` skill — the skill system, as every lifesteal is.

That is at most three affixes, one of each kind, which is what a rare has. Affixes the engine has no word for, or
that would need a second figure on one item (health on a ring, crit, move speed, a skill's rank), wait for **E43**
(a `Loot` carries several figures) and **E44** (crit and move speed as figures); nothing is shown that does not act.

## Drops

A monster's death draws once from `world.random()` for whether it drops (`LootDrops.DropPercent` 15, a boss 100, an
elite `EliteDropPercent` 40) and once more for a seed; everything else — potion or gear, rarity, base, affixes — is
drawn from that seed. A boss drops `BossDrops` (2) things. The item level is the monster's level, a boss's
`BossLevel` (1) more. `PotionPercent` (35) of ordinary drops are a potion fit for the level. Gold is unchanged.

A map's placed thing may name a base, a unique, a potion or `Xazina` (treasure: a chest-weighted roll at the floor's
level) beside the old `LootItem`s.

**On the ground** a dropped item is the chest, as before, and a magic, rare or epic one stands in a column of light
of its colour (`LootBeamMagic`, `LootBeamRare`, `LootBeamEpic`, played on it every `BeamFrames`, as a quest's glow
is). Common lies dark.

## Potions and the belt

Four potions (`Potion` blocks): hayot shishasi (35 % health), katta hayot shishasi (65 %, from level 7), mana shishasi
(40 % mana), tiklanish eliksiri (40 % both, from level 5). A potion is a stack in the backpack: its `Value` is how many
(up to `Stack`), and stacks of one potion join into the first as they come in. The **belt** is the first four stacks
of the backpack, in order; keys **1–4** drink from them (a `Drink` order, so a party drinks on every machine), with a
shared cooldown `BeltFrames` (30 = one second). The HUD draws the four quick slots above the bottom panel with their
counts, keys and the cooldown.

## The card

The engine's bag card speaks in one colour. The game paints its own over it (`ItemCard`, the same lines in the same
place, so it covers it whole): the name in the rarity's colour, the rarity word and item level, the base figure in
white, the affixes in blue, a unique's lore in gold, and **Kiyilgan bilan solishtiring** — each figure's difference to
what is worn in that slot, green for more and red for less. The same card shows for a thing on the floor and a ware
in the trade window. (E45 asks the engine for coloured card lines, so the overlay can go.)

## The merchant

His `Stock` keeps the old things and adds the potions; `Gear = 6` more are rolled at the hero's level with the shop's rarities each time a
map stocks him. A generated thing's price is its base's price × its rarity's (100/250/500/1200 %) × 8 % a level past
the first; it sells for the Trade's `SellPercent` of that, a potion stack for its potions'.

## Saving

Nothing new is written: each item's id already is the roll. A save of an older version has only the old ids, which
load as they did.

## Engine requests

- **E43** — a `Loot` carries affixes: a list of (figure, value) that `LootBag.total` and `extra` add beside its own;
  then rares get their fourth affix and any affix may roll on any slot.
- **E44** — crit chance and move speed as `Stat` words the hero's figures read; a worn thing raising a skill's rank.
- **E45** — the bag's card in colours: a line's colour from the game, so the game's `ItemCard` overlay can go.
