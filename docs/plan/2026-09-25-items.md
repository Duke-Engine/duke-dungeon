# Items: a bag to carry them in, a hand to put them down

A monster that dies leaves a thing lying on the floor three times in ten, and the hero is sent to fetch it
rather than walking over it. What he carries counts while he carries it, and he can put it down again. Built on
seams the engine already has — the context order a click on a thing gives (`DukeGame.contextOrder`), an errand
any later order gives up (`Errand`), a ground aim armed by the game (`Duke3D.aim`), and the game's own canvas
(`Painter`, `CanvasInput`) — with nothing added to the engine.

## The rules

- **Drops:** 30% from a monster, always from the boss (`LootDrops` in `data/world/world.duke`). What drops is
  drawn from the run's seed and the monster's id, as before.
- **On the floor** a thing is a chest (`GroundItem`), selectable so the pointer finds it, owned by the dungeon so
  it is nobody's hero's to box-select. Walking over it does nothing.
- **Picking up:** a right click on it with his hero selected. The client sends the game's word for that click
  (`PickUp`, the pointer of that name shows over one); the hero walks there and takes it if his bag has room. A
  full bag leaves it lying and says so.
- **The bag** has six slots (`Slots`). A thing counts for as long as it is in it: attack and armour percent, flat
  health and mana, whole points of an attribute — Strength, Agility and Intelligence items now ship.
- **Putting down:** a right click on a slot takes the thing in hand (the pointer becomes `Drop`), a left click on
  the floor sends him there to put it down; a right click or Escape changes his mind. A place he cannot reach
  has it put down as near as he got.
- **Pointing at a thing** — in the bag, or on the floor, whatever is selected — says its name, what it gives in
  the game's own words (`+3 Kuch`, `+8% Zarba`) and how to take it. What the pointer is on is the window's own
  word (`DukeGame.getPointedAt`); what lies there is read off the world after every frame.
- **Health and mana are room, not a draught.** A heart raises his maximum and fills none of it; putting it down
  takes the room away. A level still lifts his health and mana with their ceilings, and a new body arrives whole.
  Otherwise putting a heart down and picking it up again would be a heal at will.

## Orders

`PickUp(player, item)` and `DropItem(player, slot, place)` travel as `GameOrder`s like every other order of the
game, so a party's machines carry them out on the same frame. Both start an `ItemErrand` on the player's hero: a
module that walks him there and hands the thing over on arrival, given up by any later order — the engine's walk,
attack and stop, the game's hold and attack-move — or by his legs being sent anywhere else.

## The screen

`BagScreen` draws the bag at the right of the window (the client's own bag block, which can only be looked at, is
off the bar), and takes the clicks that land on it. It is told of every match it is drawn over — the one the
window opens with and a party's that replaces it — and hands that match the pickup word and the drop press.

## Three alike join

What the dungeon leaves is the hero's three attributes, +3 each (`Gauntlet`, `Boots`, `Tome`). Three of the same
thing at the same level in one bag join into one of the next level, up to `TopLevel` (3): worth the three of them
together, and from the second level bringing its `Extra` — health a second for strength, mana a second for
intelligence, quicker blows for agility — which every later level also triples:

| | level 1 | level 2 | level 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gauntlet | +3 Strength | +9, +2 health/s | +27, +6 health/s |
| Boots | +3 Agility | +9, +20% attack speed | +27, +60% attack speed |
| Tome | +3 Intelligence | +9, +2 mana/s | +27, +6 mana/s |

The join happens as the third comes in (`LootBag.take`), so it needs no room of its own, and may join again at the
level above. Attack speed is a percent because two percent would take at most one frame off a blow that comes every
24 to 34; it reaches the weapon through the engine's `RateOfFireModifier` (`level/AttackSpeed`). Things found deeper
are not worth more (`ValuePercentPerDepth = 0`): what joins has to be alike.
