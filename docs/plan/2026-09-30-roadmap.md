# Roadmap: harder monsters, new skills, and the key to the keep

The owner's list after the boss's keep landed: the keep's key and the missions that lead to it, a handful of balance
changes, new effects and skills for the dungeon's mages, and monsters that grow the way heroes do — levels, mana,
skills that open with a level, an ultimate. Too much for one spec. Six pieces, simplest first, each with its own spec,
plan and code; each leaves the game playable.

What the code does today, measured before this was written (so the pieces below start from the truth):

- **A monster's level is its floor's depth.** Every monster on a floor shows the same number (`|deep=` in the status
  line; the client's `UnitBarReading.levelOn`). There is no per-monster level.
- **Monsters have no mana, and cast only their first skill** (`Monster.skillKey`, rank fixed at 1).
- **The "necromancer mage" is `SkeletonSummoner`** (the boss named Necromancer is another creature). The healer is
  `SkeletonHealer`, the fire mage `SkeletonMage`, and the mage that only mends itself quickly is `Revenant`.
- **Heroes carry no `StatusUpdate`,** so nothing can slow or stun them. The engine already has the stun
  (`ObjectStatus.DISABLED`: no move, no weapon) and the root (`HELD`).
- **Damage the game deals itself does not say who dealt it** (`ArrowUpdate.strike`, `SkillBook`); the engine weapon
  does. Lifesteal is game code either way.
- **An effect can ride a unit for a few seconds with no engine change:** `world.effect(name, thing)` and an AURA layer
  with `Seconds`.

## 1. Balance, by data (the simplest)

- Enemy fireballs twice as fast: the fire mage's skill (`ProjectileSpeed 60 → 120`) and every enemy's ordinary fire
  (`Bow Speed`: the fire mage and the Revenant `95 → 190`, the Necromancer boss `105 → 210`).
- Every mage from the first floor: `MinDepth = 1` for SkeletonMage, SkeletonHealer, SkeletonSummoner and Revenant.
- Items drop half as often: `DropPercent 30 → 15`; the boss still always leaves one.
- The mage hero's Frost Nova: area `Radius 40 → 20`, cast `Range 80 → 40`; his Meteor: cast `Range 110 → 55`.

Data only; tests that pinned the old numbers follow them.

## 2. Stun, lifesteal, and a necromancer's four

- **Stun.** A skill may stun what it hits (`StunFrames`); the fire mage's fireball stuns for a second. Heroes get a
  `StatusUpdate`, the hit sets `DISABLED` for the frames, a stunned caster casts nothing, and stars ride the stunned
  unit for as long (an Effect with an AURA layer, played with `world.effect`).
- **Lifesteal on every boss.** A passive skill in the skill system, `LIFESTEAL`, that gives its bearer back a share of
  the damage its blows deal, heard where the game's blows land (`Swing`, `ArrowUpdate.strike`); every effect the owner
  asks for in this piece is a skill as well.
- **Summons of several kinds.** `Summons` becomes a list of kinds and counts; the summoner raises two swordsmen and two
  archers (Skeleton, Stalker) a cast.

Game code and data; no engine change.

## 3. The key to the keep (designed and agreed)

The keep stands on the ground, not a storey up; its gate never opens by itself. A right click on it flashes the
yellow ring (as a chest's does), the hero walks up and says, in a bubble over his head, that he must find the key.
The mission: kill every monster outside the keep; the last one leaves the key (a key model where a chest would lie);
picked up into the bag; a left click on it there and then on the gate, and the hero gives it to the gate, which opens.
A tracker at the top of the screen says the step; the bubble says the hero's lines. The four mages stand at the
court's corners on every floor — two summoners, two healers. The key is the first usable item: an item may carry a
`Use` (only `UNLOCK` for now; a blink and illusions later). The owner's `key_crown.glb`, its bag icon rendered from the
model by Blender.

Game code and data; no engine change.

## 4. Monsters that grow like heroes

- **A level for every monster,** rising with the floor's depth faster than it does now, and within a floor with the
  way to the boss: on the first floor the monsters before the keep stand at level 7 or 8. Health, damage and
  experience follow the level.
- **Mana and skills by level.** A monster has a mana pool and several skills, each opening at a level (the same
  `LevelPerRank` field the heroes' use) and paid for in mana; its brain chooses among the open ones.
- **The fire mage's ultimate:** a falling fireball (a `METEOR` skill of its own), open from level 6.
- **Engine:** the unit bar shows one level per floor today; it has to show each unit's own. A request to the engine
  session (as E1–E6 were), with the game sending each monster's level.

## 5. The mages' new skills and auras

- **The summoner's haste:** +75% attack speed for five seconds on the strongest ally near it, or itself (a new
  `HASTE` effect over the attack-speed seam the heroes' boots already use).
- **Auras — passive skills that hold for everyone near:** the summoner's +50% attack, the healer's 5 mana a second
  (it needs monsters' mana, from 4), the Revenant's 10% lifesteal (it needs the lifesteal of 2).

Game code and data.

## 6. What is left for later

A second usable item (a blink dagger over `BLINK`, illusions over `SUMMON`), more missions on the chain, and
whatever the owner's play of 1–5 turns up.
