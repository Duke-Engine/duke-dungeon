# Crafting (the owner's design, 2026-10-15)

Item crafting by recipe, as in Warcraft III's and Dota's recipes -- particular things put together make a better
one -- with Eternal Return's feel: the hero sits down and works it for two or three seconds, then the thing drops into
his bag. Every number below is a stand-in the owner may change in data (`data/world/crafting.duke`, the new `Potion`
and `Unique` blocks of `data/world/loot.duke`, the new things of `data/world/food.duke`); none needs Java.

## The rules

- Anywhere, no bench (Eternal Return): the **Hunar** screen on **K** lists the recipes he knows. A click on one he
  has everything for is the `Craft` order: he stops, plays his `Craft` clip (each hero's `Cook`, Quaternius'
  Fixing_Kneeling -- kneeling over his work) for the recipe's frames, the hammer ringing each second.
- A move order, a step (WASD, a dodge), a cast or a blow taken calls it off and nothing is spent -- the food work's
  own busy state (`Camp`), which crafting is one more kind of.
- Done: what it takes leaves the backpack (never what he wears), the gold his purse, and the thing goes into his bag
  -- or at his feet if the bag is full -- with a short jingle.
- What a recipe makes is a base, a unique or a stack of a material. A base is rolled as any found thing is (its
  affixes and name), its rarity drawn by the recipe's `Rarity` (out of 1000, common/magic/rare/epic; an epic on a
  base is a rare), at the hero's level or the recipe's `Level`, whichever is more. The roll is seeded from the map's
  seed, the frame and the player: the same on every machine, and a save keeps it in the thing's id as ever.
- Lock-step: crafting is an order (`craft`, its number the recipe's place in the file) the simulation checks and
  carries out on every machine.

## Knowing a recipe

- `Known = Yes`: known from the start (the basics).
- Every other is learnt: a **Hunar chizmasi** (a recipe scroll) taken into the bag teaches the first recipe of the
  file he does not yet know and is used up; with none left to learn it stays a thing to sell. Merchants sell it and
  monsters now and then drop it. A quest teaches a particular one by setting the flag `hunar:<recipe>` (a trigger's
  `SetFlag`).
- A recipe with a `Hero` is that hero's own (signature): the others never see it.
- What he knows is the run's flags, so a save keeps it.

## Writing a recipe

    Recipe
      Name = olovli_tigh          the flag's name and the save's; lower case
      Makes = olovli_tigh         a base, a unique or a Potion (a material)
      Count = 1                   a material's how many
      Rarity = [0, 700, 280, 20]  a base's rarity, drawn out of 1000; none: common
      Level = 0                   the least item level it is made at
      From = ["#qilich", "olov_toshi 2"]
      Gold = 0
      Frames = 75                 30 a second
      Known = No
      Hero =                      blank for every hero
    End

A part of `From` is a name and how many (1 if left out): a material by its Potion name; a thing by its base's or
unique's name; `*Slot` any thing worn in that slot (`*Uzuk`); `#tag` any of a `Tags` line's bases
(`qilich = xanjar qisqa_qilich ...`). A thing's part may ask a least rarity after a `/`: `*Uzuk/M 2` is two rings of
magic or better. The least rare that fits is taken first, so a rare ring is never spent where a magic one does.

## The Hunar screen (K)

One of the player screens (C, L, M and now K): the game's own, drawn on the canvas, one open at a time, Esc shuts
it, the world waits while it is open, played alone. A row a recipe he knows: the thing's picture and name in its
rarity's colour, what it takes -- each part green when he has it, red when not -- its gold and seconds. A recipe he
can make now is lit. The pointer on a row shows the thing's card beside it (E45's colours, compared with what he
wears); a click on a lit row crafts it and shuts the screen, so he is seen at work. Under the list: how many recipes
there are to learn yet.

## Sound

Each second of work the hammer (`work.craft`: the metal hits, CC0); done, `craft.done` (the sword's ring, CC0).
