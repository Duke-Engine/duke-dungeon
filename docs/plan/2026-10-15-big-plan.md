# Last Hero — the big plan (2026-10-15)

The owner, 2026-10-15: "we must grow the game; make a big plan; usage is left over and I have no time." So this plan is
run autonomously, a few streams at a time. No reviewer agents: tests plus the owner's playtest of each MSI are the check.
Anything only he can decide is marked **(owner)**; until he decides, a stand-in is used and named as one.

## Where we are

Mechanics are mostly done:
- heroes with skills, slots and a backpack;
- dialogue, quests, triggers, cutscenes and saving;
- people, gold and trade;
- the medallion and the turning, and the final fight;
- the open world with castles, Baba Yaga's hut, water, haze and the explored fog;
- the editor (paint, scatter, zones, place piece).

What is missing is mostly **content**, plus a few player-facing screens, items worth finding, and sound.

## Phase 1 — playable spine (now)

1. **Act 1, start to the turning, as one playable chain** (stand-in names and models where the owner hasn't chosen):
   - the opening battle map (armies, the hero's landing);
   - the capital with the queen and the commander;
   - the training descent;
   - the war (keeps);
   - the castle boss;
   - the medallion's turning;
   - a cutscene at each joint.
2. **Items worth finding** (Diablo-like):
   - weapon and armour items for every worn slot, at rarities common / magic / rare / epic, with random affixes from data;
   - drop tables by level, using the icon library;
   - potions on a belt bound to 1–4;
   - the merchant's stock drawn from the same tables.
3. **Player screens:**
   - character sheet (C);
   - quest log (L);
   - world map (M, from the world picture);
   - tooltips that compare an item with the worn one;
   - all in Uzbek.

## Phase 2 — Act 2 and the world

4. **Act 2 as quests in the open world:**
   - the heroine's arrival;
   - rumours in the capital;
   - Baba Yaga and the caged girl (her side quest);
   - freeing the lands (the forts);
   - the queen's castle;
   - the throne-hall interior;
   - the final fight;
   - the ending.
5. **Monsters for the world:** families per biome (forest, desert, highlands, marsh), elites with affixes, and camp bosses.
   Models come from CC0 packs **(owner downloads, or allows me to)**.
6. **World dressing:** villages, roads with travellers, merchants in towns, fast travel between discovered towers.

## Phase 3 — feel and finish

7. **Sound:** music per region and menu, and SFX for hits, skills, coins, UI. Sources are CC0/CC BY (Kenney, OpenGameArt)
   **(download permission)**.
8. **Feel:** hit-stop, screen shake tuning, damage numbers (like the coins' +N), death animations everywhere.
9. **Performance and robustness:** profile the open world, load times, memory; saves fuzzed against corruption.
10. **Steam readiness:**
    - the title "Last Hero" on splash and menu;
    - credits;
    - a settings pass;
    - the installer name;
    - a store-page shot list **(owner: names, final title)**.
