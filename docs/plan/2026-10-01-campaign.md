# Last Hero — the campaign

The story the owner settled on 2026-10-01, in two acts with two written heroes, and what the game needs to tell it.
Choices still his are marked **default** and hold until he says otherwise.

**2026-10-03, the owner:** the king is a **queen**, hungry for youth and beauty (the souls keep her young); the game
is **Uzbek first** (other languages are a later localisation); **free assets only** for now.

## The story

**Act 1 — the swordsman.** A battlefield before the kingdom's walls: the kingdom's soldiers, led by a young woman
commander, are losing; at the last she stands alone, and the enemy's commander leaps at her. The world stops. A beam
of light strikes from the sky and a young man lands in it, knocking the enemy commander flying. He is bewildered — he
was training with *her* (the woman he loves; her name is the first name the player hears) a moment ago, and now he is
here. The enemy commander rises and charges again; the commander, on her feet now, cuts him down. She tells the young
man he is a hero, that this world has none left, that he is the last one summoned to save it, and takes him to the
queen. The queen welcomes her guest, tells him how the kingdom stands, and gives him money and a medallion: it draws in the
soul of every enemy its wearer kills and pours that strength into him; only a hero can wear it. If he kills the
villain, he may go home.

The queen sends him to train before the war: a place where he can grow (the first time through, the only way open —
the war needs a hero of level 20; the levels run to 100). Then the war: he frees the conquered lands one keep at a
time, the medallion drinking every death, until he reaches the villain's castle and kills him. The medallion draws in
the villain's soul — and the curse with it. He becomes the new villain, bound to the castle he stands in; the
medallion vanishes from his neck and appears in the queen's hand, and he understands who the true villain is. Unable to
leave the castle, he raises the enemy commander the young woman killed and sends him to raze the kingdom.

**Interlude.** The risen commander takes back every land the hero freed and marches on the kingdom (**default:** a
cutscene; a playable "evil commander" chapter is for a later update).

**Act 2 — the archer.** Years later by her world's count, the young woman he was training with arrives. Nobody
summoned her: she searched for years for the world he vanished into and came to find him. She does not go to the
queen's castle and never speaks to the queen; she walks the city looking for him. The world is open to her: she roams,
grows, talks to people. They say nobody knows how old the queen is — only a creature living deep in the forest, in a
cave, might know. She fights her way to it; it tells her the queen's secret and the medallion's power (the souls it
gathers keep the queen young and beautiful for ever, and the cursed hero chained in the castle keeps the war going so that the
kingdom keeps summoning heroes), and how to end it: kill the queen.

**The end.** In the queen's hall it shows that the queen commands the cursed hero wholly; she calls him to his side. She
beats the cursed hero but does not kill him — she kills the queen. The medallion breaks, the souls it held rise, the
curse lifts, and the villain is himself again: her young man. A happy ending.

## Why it works

- The medallion turns the game's own growth into the tragedy: every kill that makes the player stronger is the curse
  coming on. Show it: a soul rising from each slain enemy into the hero.
- He wants to go home to her; she comes to find him. The first minutes name her, so act 2's arrival lands.
- She never takes the medallion — she never meets the queen — so the trap that took him cannot take her.
- The final boss she must not kill is the player's own act-1 hero, with the level, skills and things the player gave
  him.

## How it is played

- **Act 1** is linear, about 1–1.5 hours: the opening battle (a scripted fight of armies — the engine's strength), the
  hero's arrival, the city and the queen (talk, money, the medallion), the training zone (today's generated descent),
  the war (today's keeps: clear outside → key → gate → boss), the castle's boss, the turning.
- **Act 2** is open, about 2–3 hours: the one seamless world (the engine's E10), the city and its people, rumours, the
  forest cave and the creature, the lands to free again, the queen's castle, the final fight in two phases (the cursed
  hero beaten but spared, then the queen).
- Levels run to 100 (today's hero cap is 15 and monsters are levelled by depth: both to be rebalanced). Act 1's war
  asks for level 20 (**default**).

## What exists and what is new

Exists: heroes with skills, mana and levels; monsters with levels, auras and haste; bosses in keeps behind a gate a key
opens; missions with a tracker and lines said in bubbles; loot and the bag; the generated descent; the engine's
seamless world, sleeping monsters, sector routes, minimap round the hero; the main menu's spec.

New:
1. **Story scripting** — a chapter's triggers (enter a place → a line; a boss falls → the next chapter), the world
   paused for a beat, scripted moves (the hero's landing, the commander's leap, a knock-back).
2. **Cutscenes** — the camera taking a path, letterbox, lines spoken with portraits.
3. **Dialogue** — a box with a portrait and a name, a few lines, no choices needed.
4. **People and the city** — NPCs that talk, a merchant, money.
5. **Saving** — a campaign save, and what act 1 left for act 2 (the hero's build for the final boss).
6. **The medallion** — an item only a hero wears; the soul seen rising to it; the turning (the hero's look darkened,
   his side changed, the medallion gone).
7. **The final fight** — a boss that cannot be killed, only beaten; the queen's second phase.
8. **Art** — the models above, the cave, the castle, the kingdom's city; their animations.
