Modified for Last Hero (duke-dungeon) by art/huntress/export_huntress.py:
pedestal and banner removed, and the arrow held in her hand; the skeleton laid back
onto the body it skins; decimated to ~24,500 triangles; bones pruned from 438 to 62
(the cloth chains' weights moved to the hips), at most 4 bone influences a vertex;
textures cut to their base colour and downsized to 1024 (512 for hair, quiver and
arrows); a hand bone (handslot.l) added for a bow, the bow itself being KayKit's
(CC0) and attached in the game, not in this file; KayKit's character animations
(CC0) retargeted onto her skeleton (animations/characters/huntress.glb);
converted to glb.

Updated 2026-10-10: a pale violet-grey iris, pupil and highlight painted into her eyes
(eyes.jpg here is that picture); her skirt reweighted to follow her thighs; her fingers
curled in every clip (bow hand gripping, draw hand hooked while shooting); clips from
KayKit (CC0) and Quaternius' Universal Animation Library (CC0) retargeted onto her
(huntress_clips.glb).
