Modified for Duke Dungeon: glasses removed (eyes painted into his skin in their place), sword
attached to his hand, textures downsized, converted to glTF -- by art/lange/export_lange.py.

His Attack, Hurt, Death and cast clips are retargeted onto his rig (art/lange/retarget.py) from
Quaternius' Universal Animation Library (CC0) and KayKit's Character Animations (CC0).

models/characters/heroes/hero_lange.glb (2026-11-05) is the same model on the game's 55-bone rig, built in the
workspace as models/hero_lange: his rig merged into the game's with an added hand bone, the sword shortened and moved to
his right hand, the shield moved to his left arm, brows and lids painted, hair and tunic recoloured, textures downsized to
2048. His clips Walk, Run, Sprint, Dodge and Death are legs and body retargeted from Quaternius' Universal Animation
Library (CC0); Idle, Hurt and every Attack_* are made for this game.
